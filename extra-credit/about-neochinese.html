About Me

Introduction

I am deeply passionate about data science and building useful software. I'm a Computer Science student at UC San Diego with a growing interest in Machine Learning and Software Engineering.

I enjoy building projects that combine practical engineering with thoughtful design, whether that's developing an AI-powered application, creating a recommendation system, or improving the user experience of a web application.

One thing you'll quickly notice about me is that I like breaking complex problems into simple, manageable steps. Whether I'm debugging code, preparing for interviews, or learning a new technology, I naturally organize information into systems that are easier to understand and improve.

I believe good software isn't just about making something work—it's about making it understandable, maintainable, and genuinely useful to the people who use it.

Art & Illustration

Outside of engineering, color is basically my other language. I could spend an entire evening just testing how three shades of pink look next to each other before I've drawn a single line. Digital painting is where I get to be messy and intuitive in a way that debugging code never lets me be—there's no compiler yelling at me if a color choice doesn't quite work, I just try another one.

Painterly Studies

These are studies in a heavier, more painterly rendering style—built up in layered strokes rather than flat blocks of color, closer to traditional paint than line-and-fill illustration.

Honkai: Star Rail Fan Art

Honkai: Star Rail is one of my favorite games, and its character writing and world-building have genuinely inspired a lot of my art. I've drawn fan art of several characters I love, including Welt (fondly nicknamed "老杨"/"Old Yang" by the fandom) and Yao Guang, among others.

Sketches

Quick, loose sketches—less polished, but usually where I actually figure out an idea before committing to a full piece.

Flat Color Style

I went through a phase where I was completely absorbed in practicing a flat-color illustration style—clean shapes, minimal shading, and a strong emphasis on the mood a scene can carry through color alone rather than through rendering detail. I love how much atmosphere and storytelling a single flat-color scene can hold.
